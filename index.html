Manifesto

No. 04 — on why we go, and why we go slowly.

We believe the best journeys are unhurried, unremarkable to anyone else, and entirely your own — a long lunch, a wrong turn, a town with one bakery and no reason to leave.

01

Fewer places, longer stays

Three nights minimum, always. You cannot know a place from a coach window.

02

The long way is the route

Ferries over flights. Ridgelines over highways. The transit is the trip.

03

Leave it quieter

We work with 40 hosts, not 4,000. Small groups, local kitchens, no coaches.

04 — How it works

Four steps,
no forms.

No booking engine, no upsells. A person reads what you write and writes back.

01

We listen

A twenty-minute call about how you actually travel — not a checklist of countries you want to tick.

02

We draw the route

Three days later you receive a route on paper: maps, ferries, the name of a bakery, and one backup plan.

03

We book the quiet parts

Rooms, tables, and the one guide worth paying for. Everything else is left deliberately open.

04

We stay on call

One number, answered by a human, for the whole trip. Mostly for weather. Occasionally for dinner.
